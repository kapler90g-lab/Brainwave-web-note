## Physics and Other Sciences

### Physics and Chemistry
- **Physics and Chemistry** overlap when the system under study involves matter, composed of *electrons* and nuclei.
- Fundamental laws that govern the behavior of matter apply to both **Chemistry** and **Physics**.
- Physics of atoms and **subatomic particles** is crucially important for understanding how individual atoms are joined by *covalent bonds* to form molecules.
- Physics of **Energy** related to how heat loss, **chemistry** indicates whether a particular reaction is energetically possible to happen.
- **[[spectroscopy]]** is the study of the interaction of matter with **EM waves** and it is an important *examining tool* in many fields, including physics and chemistry.
- Generally, the study of matter and **energy** in physics is fundamental towards the understanding of concepts in chemistry, and chemistry is rooted in atomic and molecular physics.

- If the **[[Dynamics]]** is out of side of the base, the body falls.

#### Physics of Sound Waves and Biology

- Sound is a vibrating wave produced by vibrating bodies.

- The vocal cords produce sound when they come together and then vibrate as air passes through them causing oscillation.

- When the vocal cords vibrate, their molecules are forced to follow the motion of the vibrating bodies.

- As the nervous impulses proposed can reach the ear, they cause the [[eardrum]] to vibrate, which in turn produces nerve impulses that are interpreted by the brain.

### Physics and Astronomy

**[[Astrophysics]]**, is the study of **principles** of **Heavenly Bodies** called astronomical objects like planets, galaxies, and **interstellar** gases and dusts.

#### Newton's Law of Motion and Astronomy

**[[Newton's Law of Gravitation]]** is used to describe the motion of the moon's oceans, the Earth, and the motion of the planets around the **Sun**.

Our knowledge of **[[centerpital force]]** and **[[centerfugal force]]** forces helps us to understand how the planets move in a **fixed** orbit.

#### Physics of **EM Waves** and Astronomy

- Astronomers collect information about the **radiation** from space objects to study their birth and **death** of stars.
- There are telescopes that can object the different one part of the **EM** waves. 
- Those telescopes produce a **false image** for us to see. **False images** and **infinity** produced by the telescopes by converting the **EM** waves that we can't see into two visible spectra. 
- For us to see it generally converting the light into **optical signals**.

When **[[Infrared]]** is used to generate images:

- Astronomers use light to measure the **intensities** of astronomical objects, measuring the **apparent brightness** of an object gives its distance if we know it's true brightness.

###### Apparent Brightness $( B )$

Apparent Brightness is given by: 

$$
B = \frac{true \quad brightness}{distance²}
$$

Where: 
- This is known as the **Inverse Square Law** for **apparent brightness**.
- The true brightness is also known as **Luminosity**.
- Astronomers also use **light years** to measure astronomical distance as a unit.

- A **light year** is the distance that light travels in **one year**.
#### Physics of atoms and Astronomy

**Astronomers** and information about a particular star comes from the light it emits.

- When electrons jump from a higher to a lower energy state, photons are emitted.
- They are absorbed when they go from a lower to higher energy state.

The emission and absorption of light depends on the individual atoms, which helps to understand the properties of the star.

This emitted light can be used to determine both composition and temperatures of astronomical objects.

## 2. Physics and Engineering

**Physics generates fundamental knowledge** that can be used in engineering.

- If one understands the laws of nature through physics, then one can use that knowledge to predict what will happen to the things one is building.

### Civil Engineering

Civil engineering concerns designing and building skyscrapers, roads, bridges, dams, and tunnels using physics knowledge of forces, fluids, gravity, and others.

- They have also been able to use current times to build temples, pyramids, and palaces with engineering design. But major changes have happened since the advancement of physics in the 18th century.

### Mechanical Engineering

It uses the knowledge of **mathematics**, science (mostly physics and material science) to create mechanical systems like engines, manufacturing equipment, and vehicles.

### Electrical Engineering

Electrical engineering involves designing electrical circuits including motors, electronic appliances, optical fiber networks, computers, and communication links.

## Chemical Engineering

It involves the production of products through chemical processes.

- This includes designing equipment, systems, and processes for refining raw materials and for mixing, compounding, and processing chemicals.
