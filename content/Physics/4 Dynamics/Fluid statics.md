# Pascal's Principle

>[!Pascal's Principle] 
>states that **if a pressure is applied to an enclosed fluid, it is transmitted to every part of the fluid and the container equally**.

Mathematically,
$$\frac{F_1}{A_1} = \frac{F_2}{A_2} = P_1 = P_2$$

## Absolute, Atmospheric, Gauge Pressure

**Absolute Pressure** is the actual pressure at a given point.
$$P_s = P_{\text{atm}} + P_g$$

**Atmospheric Pressure**: the air around us has a weight that applies on the surface of the earth.                                       This is known as atmospheric pressure, which is commonly **101kPa**.
$$P_{\text{atm}} = P_s - P_g$$

**Gauge Pressure** is the pressure reading from a fluid.
$$P_g = P_s - P_{\text{atm}}$$

## Manometer

**Manometer** is an instrument used to measure pressure, usually for a gas supply.
$$P_{\text{gas}} = P_{\text{atm}} + Pgh$$

**Condition:**
$$P_1 = P_2$$
 $$P_{\text{atm}} + Pgh = P_{\text{atm}} + Pgh$$
 $$Pgh = Pgh$$
 $$Ph = Ph$$

*Note:*
- If two gases are in a manometer.
- If the **density** of one substance is known, the second can be determined from the first.

## Force in Fluids

### Buoyant Force

**Buoyant Force** is a force from the water which pushes upward against gravity.

**Gravitational Force** is the force against the buoyant force. 

**apparent Wight** This is because of the buoyant force being against the weight of the object,                              which makes it to drop in its weight.
$$W_\text{Apparent} = mg - F_b$$


>[!Archimedes' Principle]
State that; any object. wholly or particularly immersed in a fluid displaces
some amount of fluid. The weight of the displaced fluid is equal
to its buoyant force.
$$F_b = W_\text{Displaced Fluid}$$

### Floating and Sinking

**Floating or Sinking** depends on the **buoyant force** acting on the obj. and relative **density**.

- Object sinks because its weight is greater than the buoyant force.
- Object floats because its weight is equal to its buoyant force.
- Object rises because its weight is less than the buoyant force.

#### Law of Flotation

An object floats when it displaces equal amount of fluid to its weight.

- If the density of a object is greater than the density of the fluid, it will sink. (relative density > 1)
- If the density of the object is less than the density of the fluid, it will float. (relative density < 1)

$$F_b = W_\text{displaced fluid} = \text{weight of the obj.}$$
