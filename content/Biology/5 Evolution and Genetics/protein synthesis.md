# protein synthesis

**proteins** are organic molecules that are made of amino acids connected by the peptide bond.
- there are 20 different types of amino acids and the proteins they form depends upon how those amino acids are sequenced.

protein synthesis is a stepwise process that produces proteins using sequencing of the amino acids.
it involves DNA,RNA and enzymes that facilitate the overall process.

**DNA** is a molecule that mainly stores the information that is mainly required to synthesis the proteins
- it contains the instructions that helps to form a specific sequencing of the amino acids to produce different proteins

**mRNA** it contains the transcribed information from the DNA with the help of the enzyme **RNA polymerase**

**tRNA** it brings the amino acids within the cytoplasm of the cell towards the organelle that is responsible to synthesis proteins known as **ribosome**, after which binds to the mRNA forming a sequencing process.

**rRNA** this the molecule that forms the part of the ribosome that translates the instructions from the mRNA into specific sequencing of amino acids.

**ribosome** is an organelle that is responsible in translating the mRNA instructions into a polypeptide chain or a protein.

---

the process of protein synthesis involves the conversion of instructions within the DNA molecule into a product known as a protein through a **transcription** and **translation** process.

the overall flow of biological information is known as **the central dogma of life**

- generally in involves the conversion of a

     DNA $\Rightarrow$ RNA $\Rightarrow$ protein

---

### transcription

is the process by which the mRNA molecule is created by creating an exact copy of one of the strand of the DNA containing the instructions.
- transcription is the process that makes an exact copy of a section a DNA called **mRNA**

> **Messenger RNA (mRNA)** is a type of RNA synthesised during the process of transcription.
> Its role is to carry genetic information from DNA to the ribosomes (where proteins are made).
> it forms a triplets of nucleotides known as **codons**
> ![[Pasted image 20250713095844.png]]

**codons** are sequences of triplets of nucleotides and four nitrogenous bases on mRNA strand that encodes the specific sequencing of amino acids

- there are 20 different types of amino acids but the number of possible amino acid sequencing or combination is equal to $4^3 = 64$ codons

out of the 64 codons, **three codons** are **stop codons**, which stops the entire protein synthesis process those are **UAG, UAA, UGA**, and the codons that initiates the protein synthesis called **initiator or start codon** which is **AUG**
![[Pasted image 20250713095908.png]]

> **reverse transcription** is the process of synthesizing DNA molecules from an RNA template

thus during transcription:

- a small part of the DNA separates with help of **RNA polymerase**
- free RNA nucleotides attach to the complementary bases of one of the DNA templates forming mRNA and also tRNA and rRNA are transcribed from DNA

### translation

it is the process of synthesizing protein from amino acids based on the genetic information inscribed on mRNA with the help of tRNA, rRNA and ribosome.

tRNA brings the free amino acids in the cytoplasm to the organelle ribosome where the translation mainly occurs
- tRNA contains an **anticodon** that is complementary to the mRNA codons, through this base paring process the amino acids will connect together through a peptide bond creating **polypeptide chain or a protein**
![[Pasted image 20250713095952.png]]
thus during translation:

- mRNA carries the information from the nucleus to the ribosome found in the cytoplasm
- once the mRNA attaches to the ribosome the tRNA will unload its amino acid
- the tRNA anticodons will pair with the mRNAs codons in the ribosome forming a connection between the amino acids
- the info in the mRNA is translated into a protein using rRNA which is found in the ribosome
- the polypeptide chain then will be formed
